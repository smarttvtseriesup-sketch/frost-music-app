# FROST Privacy Policy

FROST is designed to minimize collection and tracking.

- The demo stores liked tracks and personalization preferences in device-local AsyncStorage.
- It has no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK.
- It does not sell personal data.
- No account or login is required for the demo.
- Production providers may receive standard network and playback information. A production deployment must publish each provider's terms and disclosures.

Network requests made by the app are limited to loading artwork and playing the configured demo source. Do not add tracking SDKs or sponsored recommendation feeds.
